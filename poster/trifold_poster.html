Computational Surrogate for Ac-225 Production Planning
in Targeted Alpha Therapy

Physics-informed neural network · Ra-226 → Ac-225 transmutation chain · 0D Bateman reference

Computational Pharmacology (PHA) · Physics-informed ML · Nuclear supply planning

Background

Actinium-225 is a scarce alpha-emitting radiopharmaceutical used in targeted alpha therapy (TAT) for cancer. Global supply limits clinical trials and patient access.

Planning irradiation scenarios — balancing neutron flux, energy, and time — requires solving a stiff five-isotope transmutation chain many times. Classical ODE integrators (Radau) are accurate but too slow for large-scale parameter sweeps.

This project builds a physics-informed neural network (PINN) surrogate validated strictly against that ODE reference (NNDC/JENDL data) — not lab or hospital measurements.

Research Question

Can a physics-informed neural network accurately and rapidly predict Ac-225 inventory across diverse irradiation scenarios compared to a trusted Bateman ODE reference?

Hypothesis

If Bateman physics is embedded in the network architecture and training loss, the PINN will match the ODE reference within 10% on held-out production scenarios while enabling orders-of-magnitude faster screening than sequential ODE integration.

Expected Outcomes

  • Six independent validation gates will PASS
  • Held-out Ac-225 median error < 10% vs ODE
  • Strongest accuracy in thermal and 14 MeV regimes
  • Largest errors near epithermal and ~6.4 MeV threshold energies
  • Empty-target safety: no production from zero inventory

Methodology

  1. Reference model: 0D five-species Bateman ODE with NNDC/ENSDF half-lives and JENDL cross sections; stiff Radau integrator generates training targets.
  2. Training coverage: Scenarios across thermal (~0.025 eV), epithermal, threshold (~6.4 MeV), and fast 14 MeV neutron energies; virgin and recycled inventories.
  3. Surrogate architecture: Semi-analytic Bateman backbone with bounded neural corrections — chain physics is structural, not learned from scratch.
  4. Training curriculum: 600-epoch physics-only pretrain, then 3,400-epoch joint training (4k total budget, v63 weights); mass conservation enforced in loss.
  5. Validation suite: Six independent gates — empty-tank safety, production scenario, decay-chain ingrowth, species quality gate, PINN–ODE correlation, and 22 held-out scenarios (seed 42).
  6. Interactive demo: Streamlit app for live PINN vs ODE comparison, 10,000-point parameter screening, and speed benchmark.

Results & Conclusions

The PINN passed 6/6 independent validation gates with a 4.51% held-out Ac-225 median error vs the stiff ODE reference. The surrogate enables rapid scenario screening impractical with repeated ODE solves.

Limitations: Validation is ODE-only (not reactor or clinical assay data). This is a 0D lumped model without geometry-resolved transport or patient pharmacokinetics. Near-threshold energies remain the hardest regime — confirm critical designs with the full ODE.

Future work: Literature benchmark with faculty mentor; expanded held-out regimes; optional MCNP/OpenMC coupling for geometry effects.
